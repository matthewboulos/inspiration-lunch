# Nutrition spec (EXAMPLE -- set your own targets)

These are example targets to show the structure the skill expects. **Edit every
number to your own goals** before relying on them, then save the result as your
own private preference note (see this directory's `README.md`).

Each recommended lunch option should be a single, complete dish (or a dish the
assistant has completed for you) that meets:

- **Calorie band:** ~500-650 kcal per option.
- **Lean-protein floor:** at least ~35 g of lean protein.
- **Saturated-fat ceiling:** at most ~5 g saturated fat (estimated).
- **Soluble-fiber source:** include one most days (e.g. beans, oats, lentils,
  avocado, certain vegetables).
- **Plate ratio:** roughly 1/2 vegetables + 1/4 protein + 1/4 carbohydrate.
- **Warm preferred:** favor a warm dish over a cold one when both fit.
- **Taste matters:** it has to be something you'd actually want to eat -- do not
  sacrifice palatability to hit the macros.

**Deliverable per option:** the specific dish (by its real menu name) plus
estimated macros (calories, protein, saturated fat) so you can see it meets the
spec at a glance.

Notes you may want to add for yourself: a preferred lead protein (e.g. lean
chicken breast most days), a cap on how many of a given protein per week (e.g. at
most one salmon option per day), and any allergies or hard dislikes.
